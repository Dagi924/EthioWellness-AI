# EthioWellness 

## Project Description

**EthioWellness ** is an AI-powered wellness and lifestyle platform designed around Ethiopian foods, eating habits, fasting practices, physical activity, and everyday wellness. It provides personalized recommendations for nutrition, meals, exercise, hydration, sleep, recovery, and healthy lifestyle habits based on each user's health profile, dietary restrictions, health conditions, goals, and lifestyle.

The platform combines Ethiopian and international food data with AI-powered personalization to help users build healthier and more sustainable daily routines.

## Problem

Most wellness and fitness applications are designed around foreign food databases, lifestyles, and health practices. They often lack Ethiopian foods such as **injera, shiro, and tibs**, provide limited support for Ethiopian Orthodox and Ramadan fasting practices, and treat nutrition, exercise, hydration, sleep, and recovery as separate activities.

This makes it difficult for users to receive wellness recommendations that actually fit their culture, lifestyle, dietary practices, and personal needs.

**EthioWellness ** addresses these limitations by combining Ethiopian and international food data with AI-powered recommendations across multiple areas of wellness.

## Main Features

* Personalized wellness and health profile
* Ethiopian and international food database
* Food logging through camera, voice, or manual entry
* Nutrition and nutrient tracking
* AI wellness and nutrition chatbot
* Personalized meal planning
* AI-powered exercise planning
* Meal and exercise integration
* Ethiopian Orthodox and Ramadan fasting support
* Hydration planning and tracking
* Sleep and recovery tracking
* Daily personalized wellness plans
* Healthy habit recommendations
* Grocery planning
* Smart cooking recommendations
* Nutrient deficiency risk monitoring
* Wellness progress and activity tracking
* Nutritionist supervision and support
* Amharic and English interface
* Light and dark themes

## Wellness Approach

**EthioWellness ** connects different aspects of a user's daily wellness instead of treating them independently.

```text
                    User Profile
                         │
                         ▼
                  AI Wellness Engine
                         │
        ┌────────────────┼────────────────┐
        ▼                ▼                ▼
    Nutrition         Exercise          Fasting
        │                │                │
        └────────────────┼────────────────┘
                         │
            ┌────────────┼────────────┐
            ▼            ▼            ▼
       Hydration       Sleep       Recovery
            │            │            │
            └────────────┼────────────┘
                         ▼
                Personalized Daily
                  Wellness Plan
```

The goal is to provide recommendations that adapt to the user's overall lifestyle rather than focusing on a single metric such as calories or exercise.
